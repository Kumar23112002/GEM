Our Story

Solving a Problem We Lived Through

In 2023, our founding team was running a small agri-export business from Ludhiana. We spent months navigating unreliable brokers, opaque pricing, and constant payment disputes. Deals that should've taken days took weeks.

We knew there had to be a better way. So we built Goeazymart — a platform where verified buyers and sellers meet directly, negotiate transparently, and transact securely. No middlemen taking 15-20% cuts. No hidden pricing. Just a clean, transparent marketplace.

Today, we serve 15,000+ businesses across 50 countries — but our mission hasn't changed: make global trade simple, fair, and accessible to everyone.

Gurpreet Singh

Co-Founder & CEO

What Drives Us

Our Mission & Vision

Everything we build is guided by a clear purpose and a bold vision for the future of trade.

Our Mission

To empower businesses of every size with direct access to global commodity markets — removing intermediaries, eliminating information asymmetry, and building trust through technology and transparency.

Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted B2B marketplace for commodities — where any business, from a small farmer in Punjab to a large retailer in Dubai, can trade with confidence and grow without limits.

Our Journey

From Idea to Impact

A look at how far we've come since we started building in 2023.

2023 · Q1

The Spark

Our founders, frustrated by archaic trade processes, sketch the initial concept for Goeazymart on a whiteboard in Ludhiana.

2023 · Q3

First 100 Users

After 6 months of building, we launch our MVP with 100 early-adopter buyers and sellers. First transaction: ₹45,000 of Basmati Rice.

2024 · Q1

Seed Funding

Raised our seed round to expand the platform, hire our team, and build advanced RFQ + negotiation tools.

2024 · Q3

Going Global

Expanded to 25 countries with localized payments, currencies, and 24/7 multilingual support.

2025 · Q2

10,000 Users Milestone

Crossed 10,000 verified buyers and sellers. Launched mobile apps and added hard commodities (metals, minerals).

2025 · Q4

Today — and Beyond

Now serving 15,000+ businesses across 50+ countries with ₹240 Cr+ in annual GMV. And we're just getting started.
