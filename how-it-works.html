Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about trading on Goeazymart.

How much does it cost to use Goeazymart?
Registration is completely free for both buyers and sellers. Goeazymart charges a small commission (2-5% depending on category) only on successful transactions. There are no listing fees, subscription fees, or hidden charges.
How do I know if a seller is trustworthy?
Every seller goes through a strict KYC verification process where we validate their business registration, GST, PAN, and bank details. Verified sellers show a green checkmark on their profile. You can also see seller ratings, reviews from past buyers, and completed order history.
What happens if I don't receive my order?
Payment is held in escrow and only released to the seller once you confirm delivery. If there's a dispute (product not received, quality mismatch, damaged goods), you can raise a dispute within 7 days. Our team mediates and issues refunds if the seller is at fault.
Can I buy small quantities or only bulk?
Both! Retail products can be bought in single units through our standard e-commerce checkout. Bulk commodities require an RFQ (Request for Quote) where you specify your required quantity and negotiate with sellers.
How long does shipping take?
Shipping timelines vary by product and location. Domestic retail orders typically deliver in 3-7 days. Bulk commodity shipments take 15-45 days depending on destination, quantity, and shipping method. Each order shows the estimated delivery date.
What payment methods are supported?
We support credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), UPI, net banking, and bank wire transfers for large transactions. All payments are processed through PCI-DSS compliant gateways (Stripe, Razorpay).
