Quick Answers

Some of the most common questions we receive.

How fast do you respond?

We respond to all inquiries within 24 business hours. Support chat is available 24/7 for urgent matters.

I forgot my password. Help!

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and we'll email you a secure reset link within minutes.

How do I become a seller?

Click "Register" at the top, select "Seller" as your account type, and complete the multi-step onboarding. Our team verifies you within 24-48 hours.

Can I request a demo?

Absolutely! Contact our sales team by selecting "Bulk / Enterprise Sales" in the form above.

I have an order issue. What now?

Go to your Buyer Dashboard → My Orders → select the order → click "Need Help". This creates a priority ticket for our team.

Do you have a mobile app?

Our mobile apps for iOS and Android are launching soon. In the meantime, our website is fully mobile-optimized.
